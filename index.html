Research Framework: Donor Segmentation Project (Monitor Institute by Deloitte, July 2025)

The research found that no single attribute predicts which donors will deepen their relationship. Its recommendation is to track a set of attributes and combine data with steward judgment. OCF's model follows that approach: the system recommends a placement, the steward adjusts it, and leadership approves any portfolio move.

Five lessons and how this model applies them

LessonHow OCF applies it
Segmentation can't be one-size-fits-allOCF segments, service layers, and steward engagement ratings, with adjustable thresholds
People, process, and technology must work togetherThe system places funds, stewards rate engagement, dashboards are governed, and AI supports Digital Stewardship
Fundraising skill alone isn't enoughDirectors and Highly Active / Legacy focus on advising, family facilitation, and legacy planning
Balance donor interests with community focusMeet donors where they are, then connect them to community knowledge (Community Connectors)
Getting dollars out can bring dollars inGrantmaking prompts for inactive and zero-balance funds, and an active-grantmaking KPI

Portfolio management

  • Assign low-touch donors to staff focused on operational engagement
  • Review and rebalance portfolios once a year
  • Stewards focus on the top 20% of their portfolio

Engaging donors who need less support

  • Put as many funds as possible in lower-touch groups, then use targeted content so engaged donors move up on their own
  • Welcome series after a donor joins the mailing list
  • A strong newsletter clickthrough rate triggers personal outreach

Attributes to add next

  • Age and family composition (CRM)
  • Net worth (Experian, June 2025)
  • Giving and grant totals (donations and grants exports)
  • Profile type and org roles for Advisor Champions
  • Opportunities for Recruiting

Source: Donor Segmentation Project, Final Research Report (Monitor Institute by Deloitte for Seattle Foundation, July 2025).

Digital Stewardship · Marketing / Operations + AI

Digital Stewardship is mainly for Emerging Generosity funds (Charitable Checkbook, giving circles, OVG, new relationships) and low-balance funds. Marketing and Operations run it with AI support, and a person steps in when a fund shows a reason to move up.

AI may

  • Recommend approved content matched to interests
  • Summarize relationship history
  • Detect changes in activity or inactivity
  • Flag missing CRM data
  • Draft follow-ups for staff review
  • Flag funds that may be ready to move up

AI may not

  • Make final placements
  • Treat inferred personal circumstances as fact
  • Give tax, legal, or legacy advice
  • Change CRM records without an accountable person
  • Replace a steward in a sensitive conversation

Audiences

  • New fundholder onboarding
  • Charitable Checkbook users
  • Giving circles and OVG
  • Zero-balance and pass-through funds
  • Year-end contributors

Placement Rules

Fields used from the fund list: Fund ID (A), Name (C), Fund Group (D), Closed (G), Created (AK), Fund Segment (CS), Steward (CV), and Current Balance (DC). Opportunities (Legacy, Lifetime Funds, incoming donations, New Prospect Pipeline) and advisor-linked funds are matched to funds by fund name. Segments are assigned in this order, and the first match wins: Internal, then Legacy Builders, Impact Maximizers, Community Connectors, Emerging Generosity, and Growing Philanthropic Partners.

Recommended layer thresholds













Layer logic

  • Directors: Impact Maximizers, or any fund at or above the Directors threshold
  • Highly Active / Legacy: Legacy Builders (Nathan leads legacy), or a balance at or above the Highly Active threshold
  • Associate Advisor: balance at or above the Associate threshold
  • Digital Stewardship: everything else, plus Internal / Operational funds
Engagement signals (from the opportunity and advisor files)
  • Gift: an incoming cash or stock donation in the last 12 months
  • Legacy: an active Legacy Opportunity. A signed agreement or LOI sets the fund to at least Highly Active / Legacy
  • Advisor: a professional advisor is linked to the fund

Alignment flags

  • Move up: the recommended layer is higher-touch than the current steward's layer
  • Review: lighter touch: the fund could be served by a lower-touch layer
  • Aligned: the layers match
  • Assign steward: the fund has no steward
  • Hold: the steward isn't mapped to a layer

Directors carry funds from every layer because of history and relationships. "Review: lighter touch" is a prompt for the annual portfolio review, not an instruction.

Governance & Success Measures

Monthly

  • Stewards review Top 20% funds and next actions
  • Marketing and Operations review digital signals
  • Funds flagged "Move up" reviewed

Quarterly

  • Leadership reviews layer mix and capacity
  • Handoffs between Digital Stewardship and stewards
  • Legacy Builders and zero-balance review
  • Sample check of AI recommendations

Annually

  • Portfolio review and rebalancing
  • Segment, layer, and engagement rating reviewed for every fund
  • Thresholds recalibrated
